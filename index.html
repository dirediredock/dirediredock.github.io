Hi folks!

I'm a data scientist and PhD Candidate at the Department of Computer Science of the University of British Columbia. I build tools and research the theory and practice of 'invariant plots' (alternative network visualization) with Tamara Munzner, Stephen Kobourov, and team peers at the InfoVis Group.

I'm Chilean and Canadian, Matías Ignacio Bofarull Oddó 🇨🇱 is my long name, Matt Oddo 🇨🇦 for short, and Matt I. B. Oddo 📖 for publishing purposes.

I have interdisciplinary interests and academic background: biology BSc (2016, UBC-O), earth science MSc (2019, UBC EOAS), and computer science PhD (2027, UBC CS).

Selectoscope

An instrument to visually explore network structure, as a microscope is for tissue or a telescope is for outer space.

A familiar view (NodeLink, Labels) beside interpretable views of density (Cores), eccentricity (Hops) and decomposition (Matrix), linked by one selection.
